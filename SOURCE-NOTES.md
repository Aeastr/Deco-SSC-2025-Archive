The source was extracted from the original `DecoFinal.swiftpm.zip`, preserved at commit `6395ec3117f1b6af97f551b6ee623261ad07f521` in the original repository. Its SHA-256 is `b3a10e86785ce93de5d1f604f292a45c6eb2b3f92a97bd3823811974fda10a43`. The original ZIP remains in the local historical archive, outside this public source tree.
